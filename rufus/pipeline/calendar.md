# Rotation calendar

The generation routine takes today's row (by weekday), generates the three
briefs, and balances modes ~60/40 community-forward. Formats per the
post-format plan: Living Still ~40%, Turn ~25%, Carousel ~15%, CCTV ~10%,
Aftermath ~5%, Tentpole ~5%. Scenes must vary daily — repetition is the
distribution killer, not the AI label. Never reuse a scene brief within
14 days.

| Day | Still 1 | Still 2 | Video |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| Mon | Community errand (new scene: laundromat, DMV, bus stop, library…) | Rogue CCTV b/w frame, new location | Living Still — community, subtle motion loop |
| Tue | Community job attempt (confidently wrong) | Rogue aftermath (scene post-chaos, Rufus gone) | The Turn — community→rogue in one clip |
| Wed | Two-mode carousel pair (same setting, day/night) — counts as both stills | — | Living Still — rogue, night |
| Thu | Community + elderly bystander tolerance scene | Rogue daytime chaos w/ adult bystanders | The Turn or CCTV motion clip |
| Fri | Community errand, new setting | Rogue vending/drive-thru/counter class scene | Tentpole candidate (bigger scene, 15–20s) |
| Sat | Pivot-adjacent (pouch prominent — full artwork chain) | Rogue CCTV | Living Still — either mode |
| Sun | Two-mode carousel pair | — | Weekly 60s+ episode cut (assemble from week's clips) |

Escalation notes:
- Saturday's pouch-prominent still runs the full `sippy-artwork.py` chain.
- Tentpoles that clear the gate exceptionally well get promoted to the
  following week's pinned-post rotation.
