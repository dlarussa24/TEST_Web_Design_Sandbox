Century Slate Roof

Hand-split Welsh slate, graded and laid in diminishing courses, engineered to shelter the residence for more than a hundred years.

Copper Ridgework

Standing-seam copper ridges, valleys and gutters, folded by hand and left to earn their patina season after season.

Envelope Waterproofing

A triple-membrane building envelope seals every junction from ridge to footing, tested against wind-driven rain at storm pressure.

Heritage Timber Frame

A post-and-beam skeleton of quarter-sawn oak, joined with pegged mortise and tenon exactly as the first Meridian houses were.

Certified Craftsmanship

Every structural assembly is inspected twice — once by the master builder, once by an independent surveyor — before it is ever concealed.

Panoramic Glazing
