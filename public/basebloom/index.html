Straight answers

The questions everyone asks.

Who owns the website?

You do. The site, the domain, the photos and the copy are all yours, registered in your name. We build it and look after it; we never hold it.

What happens if I cancel the care plan?

The site is still yours. We hand over the files and access, and you can host it anywhere. Nothing goes dark and nothing is held hostage. The care plan keeps the site maintained and found, and cancelling it never switches the site off.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. The build is a one-time job and the care plan is month-to-month. Cancel whenever you like.

Do I keep my domain?

Yes. It's registered in your name, not ours. If you already own one, we connect it; if you don't, we register it for you, as you.

Who writes the words on the site?

We do. It comes out of one 20-minute call about your work and your neighborhoods, plus your photos and your reviews. You read it before anything goes live, and two revision rounds are included.

What does month two look like?

The care plan: your site stays fast and updated, your Google listing gets fresh posts and photos, your reviews get watched, and you get a one-page plain-English report showing what people searched and what moved.
