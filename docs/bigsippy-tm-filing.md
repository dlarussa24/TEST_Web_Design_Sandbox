# BIG SIPPY — trademark filing packet (Class 041)

**I cannot file this for you.** A USPTO application requires a declaration
signed under penalty of perjury *by the applicant* and payment from your own
account. This packet reduces the filing to roughly 20 minutes of form-filling
with every decision pre-made.

> **Not legal advice.** The knockout search done earlier proves nothing
> obvious blocks the mark. It is **not** a clearance opinion, and only an
> attorney's full search can give you one. For a mark you intend to put on
> merchandise, an hour of a trademark attorney's time (~$300–500) is
> defensible insurance.

---

## Why now rather than later

The mark accrues value the moment the account starts growing, and the person
most likely to file it is someone who watches you succeed. Filing is
~$250–350. Losing the name after building an audience on it is
catastrophically more expensive.

|---|---|---|
| **Mark** | `BIG SIPPY` | Exactly as used — two words, no stylization |
| **Mark type** | **Standard character** | Protects the words in any font/colour/layout. Broader than a design mark, and your logo treatment is still evolving. |
| **Class** | **041** — Entertainment services | What actually exists today: an ongoing online video series |
| **Filing form** | **TEAS Plus** if your goods/services match the USPTO's pre-approved ID Manual wording (cheaper); TEAS Standard if you customize | Plus is the lower fee but requires stricter compliance |

### Goods/services wording — Class 041

Draft, to be matched against the USPTO ID Manual before filing:

> Entertainment services, namely, providing a continuing series of comedic
> audiovisual programs and short-form videos featuring an animated animal
> character, delivered via the internet and social media platforms.

If TEAS Plus rejects custom wording, search the ID Manual for
"entertainment services... continuing series" and take the nearest
pre-approved entry.

### Filing basis — pick one

- **Section 1(a), use in commerce** — use this **once the accounts are live
  and BIG SIPPY appears in published content**. Faster to registration, no
  later fees.
- **Section 1(b), intent to use** — use this if filing *before* launch. It
  reserves your priority date now, but you must later file a Statement of Use
  with an additional fee (~$150/class).

**Recommendation:** launch the accounts first (see `launch-checklist.md`),
post for a week or two so the mark is genuinely in use, then file under 1(a).
The priority difference is small; the cost and paperwork difference is not.

### Specimen (required for 1(a))

A specimen shows the mark **as actually used in commerce** — not a mockup.
For Class 041 entertainment services, acceptable specimens include:

- A screenshot of the TikTok/Instagram profile or a published post where
  BIG SIPPY is visible and the entertainment service is being provided
- A screenshot of your website page advertising the series

The screenshot must show the URL and the date. A frame from a video where the
pouch shows the BIG SIPPY wordmark, in a published post, works well — the
pipeline's composited label makes this straightforward.

## Filing steps

1. Go to the USPTO Trademark Center (`uspto.gov` → Trademarks → Apply).
2. Create a USPTO.gov account with two-factor auth (required).
3. Start a new application → select TEAS Plus or Standard.
4. Owner information — LLC name and address, or your own.
5. Mark: `BIG SIPPY`, standard character format.
6. Class 041 with the wording above.
7. Basis 1(a) with specimen, or 1(b) with none.
8. Sign the declaration — **this is the part only you can do**.
9. Pay ~$250–350.
10. Save the serial number. Docket a check for ~3 months (first Office Action
    typically issues in 3–8 months).

## What the earlier knockout search established

- The nearest hit was **BIG SIPPER** — Class 032 (soft drinks, fruit juices),
  **abandoned in 1999** with no statement of use. Dead marks do not block.
- Nothing live surfaced for "Sippy" in the beverage space.
- **This proves absence of an obvious blocker, not clearance.** It did not
  search common-law (unregistered) uses, state registrations, or similar-
  sounding marks in adjacent classes.

## Classes deliberately not filed now

- **Class 025 (apparel)** — file this **before merch launches**, not after.
  It becomes the priority the moment you list a shirt. ~$250–350.
- **Class 032 (soft drinks)** — only if you ever make a real beverage. Two
  issues need review first: the dead BIG SIPPER mark was in this exact class,
  and "sippy" may face a descriptiveness objection for drink products.
