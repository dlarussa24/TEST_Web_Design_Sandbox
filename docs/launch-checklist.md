## Step 1 · Domain (2 min — do this first)

Register the `.com` before the social handles. It's the cheapest lock
(~$12/yr), it's instant, and it's the one asset no platform controls.

Point it at a link-in-bio page. **This is the fix for the biggest structural
gap in the playbook** — every follower is otherwise rented, and a single ban
ends the business. Even a one-page site with an email capture is an asset
nobody can delete.
